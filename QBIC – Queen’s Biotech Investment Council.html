Queen’s Biotech Investment Council

The Queen’s Biotech Investment Council (QBIC) is a student-run council at Queen’s University in Kingston. We focus on bringing together commerce, health and life sciences to guide students to research and trade public biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies.

Equity research

Coverage & publishing of student-made clinical research papers

Portfolio management

Workshops & trading volatile biopharma stocks

Competitions

Leading an inter-provincial biotech stock pitch competition

Our Team

Departments

Research

Our analysts cover a sector of biotech, read the primary trial data and build valuation models. Each semester ends with a collaborative written paper reviewed by team members.

We especially encourage health sciences, life sciences, and biology students to apply to become a research analyst.

Trading

Our traders separate into sub-teams and pitch ideas by the end of the first month, competing internally to build a viable portfolio composed of biotech and pharmaceutical companies.

This department strongly encourages commerce students to apply, however, any health science, life science, or biology major is encouraged to send in an application - especially those with trading experience or knowledge.
